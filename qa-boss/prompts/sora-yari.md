## attack_block B  (parry sweep into thrust)
BLOCK-COUNTER: she sweeps the spear across in a deflecting parry to the side, pivots, then snaps a quick

## hit  (flinch, quick recover — must read when re-triggered mid-flow)
HIT REACTION: her head and torso whip sharply backward as she takes a blow, a hard stagger that quickly
resolves back toward her guard stance. Quick whole-body flinch, NOT a slow topple. Ends at the stance.

## ko  (cause-free fall, ends on ground)

own weight and she collapses to the ground and stays down, motionless. NO blow, no impact, no flash —
only her own collapse. Ends lying/kneeling on the ground (does NOT return to standing).

## victory  (spear flourish taunt)
VICTORY: she spins the ring-spear once in a confident flourish, the rings singing, plants the butt on
the ground and straightens tall in a proud stance, then settles back to the starting stance.
